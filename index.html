FAQs

Questions? We've got answers

How is my fare calculated?

The fare depends on the distance of your trip and the type of ryd you choose. It is shown upfront before you book and includes the ride fare, rydap's small app amount and taxes where applicable. A waiting charge is added only if the ryd waits more than 3 minutes at pickup.

How do I pay?

Pay your Driving Partner directly at the end of the trip, in cash or by UPI. The amount to pay is shown in the app.

What is the waiting charge?

After your ryd reaches the pickup point, the first 3 minutes are free. After that a small per-minute charge applies (it depends on the vehicle type) and is shown live in both apps. All of it goes to the Driving Partner.

Which languages does rydap support?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada and Malayalam — in both the passenger and the Driving Partner app.

Can I cancel a ride?

Yes, you can cancel before your trip starts. Please cancel only when needed — your Driving Partner may already be on the way.

How do Driving Partners earn with rydap?

Driving Partners keep their full ride fare and all waiting charges. rydap's app amount is paid by the passenger on top of the fare and is settled from the Driving Partner's rydap wallet. There is no joining fee and no subscription.

Where is rydap available?

rydap is starting in Hyderabad for rides within the city. We will be expanding to more cities soon.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Profile → Delete Account. You can also write to support@rydap.in. More about account deletion.
