2. What rydap does

rydap is a technology platform. It helps Passengers find nearby independent Partners who offer rides by bike, auto-rickshaw or car, and helps both sides coordinate the ride (booking, pickup, live location, chat and fare information).

  • rydap does not own or operate vehicles and does not employ Partners. Rides are provided by Partners, who are independent service providers.
  • The contract for the ride itself is between the Passenger and the Partner. rydap facilitates it through the Platform.
  • rydap currently operates for rides within a city (local trips). Trips between towns and trips above the distance limit shown in the app are not accepted.

3. Eligibility and your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into a binding contract under Indian law.
  • You sign in with your Indian mobile number and a one-time password (OTP). You must use a number that belongs to you.
  • One phone per account: your account is active on one phone at a time. Signing in on another phone signs the older phone out. While a ride is in progress, signing in on another phone is not allowed until the ride ends.
  • You must give accurate information and keep it up to date. You are responsible for all activity on your account; keep your phone and OTPs private.
  • We may refuse, suspend or close accounts as described in Suspension and termination.

4. Booking a ride

  • You can book by chatting with the app or in U-Mode (choosing pickup and drop on the map). Before you confirm, the app shows the upfront fare for each ride type.
  • Your request is sent to Partners near your pickup point. If no Partner accepts within the search time, the app tells you; you may retry, change the ride type or add an extra amount to your offer.
  • When a Partner accepts, you see their name, vehicle details and live location. The ride starts only after you share your ride PIN with the Partner.
  • Please be ready at the pickup point. Make sure the vehicle number matches the one shown in the app before you board.

5. Fares, waiting charges and taxes

The total you pay for a ride is made up of:

PartWhat it is
Ride fareThe Partner's fare for the trip, based on distance, ride type and time of day. It belongs to the Partner.
App amountrydap's small platform amount for the ride, shown in the fare.
Waiting chargeAfter the Partner arrives at pickup, the first 3 minutes are free. After that, a per-minute charge for the ride type applies, up to a maximum number of minutes shown in the app. The waiting charge goes to the Partner and is shown live in both apps.
TaxesGST or other taxes where applicable under law, shown in the fare.
  • The upfront fare is shown before booking. The final amount may be higher only by the waiting charge (if any) or where the ride is changed at your request.
  • Any extra amount you choose to add while searching is added to your fare and shown before you confirm.
  • Tolls, parking or similar charges are not part of the fare unless the app shows them.
  • Fares and charges may change from time to time. The fare that applies to your ride is the one shown in the app when you book.

6. Payment

  • Passengers pay the Partner directly at the end of the ride, in cash or by UPI, the total amount shown in the app.
  • The Partner marks the ride as paid in the rydap Drive app. If you have a dispute about the amount, contact us at support@rydap.in with your ride details.
  • Where we offer online payments (for example, Partners paying their wallet dues), those payments are processed by third-party payment gateways and are also subject to their terms.

7. Cancellations

  • Passengers can cancel a ride before the trip starts. rydap currently does not charge a cancellation fee. If this changes, the fee will be shown in the app before you cancel.
  • Partners can cancel an accepted ride before the trip starts, choosing a reason in the app.
  • A ride cannot be cancelled after it has started with the ride PIN.
  • Frequent cancellations hurt the other side. Repeated or abusive cancellations may lead to warnings or limits on your account.

8. Your responsibilities as a Passenger

  • Treat Partners with respect. Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
  • Do not carry illegal, dangerous or prohibited items, and do not ask the Partner to break traffic rules.
  • Do not exceed the number of people allowed for the vehicle type. Wear a helmet on bike rides as required by law.
  • Pay the full amount shown in the app at the end of the ride.
  • You are responsible for any damage you cause to the Partner's vehicle beyond normal use.

9. Terms for driver Partners

Joining and verification

  • To drive with rydap you must hold a valid driving licence for your vehicle, a valid registration certificate (RC), valid insurance and any permit or badge required by law for your vehicle and city, and your vehicle must be roadworthy.
  • You give us your driving licence, RC and Aadhaar details during registration, and you accept these Terms in the app. We verify your identity when you sign in on a new phone. You must update your documents when they change or expire.

Providing rides

  • You are an independent service provider, not an employee or agent of rydap. You decide when to go online and which ride requests to accept.
  • Drive safely and follow the Motor Vehicles Act, traffic rules and any applicable state rules. Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Pick up the Passenger at the pickup point, start the trip only with the Passenger's ride PIN, follow a reasonable route and end the trip at the drop point.
  • Collect only the amount shown in the app. Do not ask Passengers for extra money.
  • Keep the app open (or running in the background) while online so ride requests and the Passenger's live tracking work.

Earnings and your rydap wallet

  • You keep the full ride fare and all waiting charges for every ride.
  • For each ride, the app amount and GST (where applicable) are paid by the Passenger as part of the total you collect. These amounts are settled from your rydap wallet when the trip starts and are listed ride by ride in the app.
  • If your wallet balance goes below zero, that amount is payable to rydap. You can pay it in the app. If dues stay above the limit shown in the app for longer than the allowed time, accepting new rides may be paused until they are paid.
  • Bonuses, welcome amounts and launch offers are given under the conditions shown in the app at that time. They are added to your wallet balance, cannot be withdrawn as cash, and may be changed or ended at any time.
  • You are responsible for your own income tax and any other taxes on your earnings.

10. Ratings and feedback

After a ride, Passengers can rate it and leave feedback. Ratings help us keep quality high. Low ratings or serious complaints may be reviewed and can lead to action on the account.

11. Safety and emergencies

  • rydap is not an emergency service. In an emergency, always contact 112 or the nearest authorities first.
  • Please report any safety incident to us at support@rydap.in as soon as possible.

  • You can turn off push notifications in your phone settings, but you may then miss ride updates or ride requests.
