In short: we collect what we need to run safe, fair rides — your account details, ride details and location while you use the service. Driver partners' location is used only while they are online. We don't sell your data, we don't store your in-ride chats or document photos, and you can delete your account at any time.

1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how rydap IT Solutions (“rydap”, “we”, “us”), a sole proprietorship based in India, collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the rydap passenger app, the rydap Drive app for driver partners and the website rydap.in (the “Platform”).

We process personal data in line with Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. By using the Platform you agree to this policy together with our Terms & Conditions.

3. Your phone number from the SIM

On Android, when you sign in, the app can show the phone numbers of your SIM cards so you can pick yours instead of typing it. This uses Android's own phone-number picker: we only receive the number you tap. We do not read your contacts, SMS or call history.

4. How we use location

  • Passengers: location is used to set your pickup point, show nearby rides and fares, and show your trip. We do not track you in the background when you are not using the app for a ride.
  • Driver partners: while you are online, your location is used to send you nearby ride requests, including when the app is in the background or the phone is locked. During a ride it is shared live with your passenger. When you go offline, we stop collecting it.
  • You can turn location access off in your phone settings, but the Platform cannot work without it.

5. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account (including OTP, one-phone-per-account and identity checks).
  • To match passengers with nearby driver partners and run each ride — booking, pickup, live tracking, ride PIN, fares and waiting charges.
  • To calculate partner earnings, wallet balances, dues, bonuses and taxes, and to keep accounting records.
  • To send ride, safety, account and wallet notifications.
  • To give customer support and handle complaints and disputes.
  • For safety and fraud prevention — for example, detecting fake accounts, misuse of offers or unsafe behaviour.
  • To fix crashes, improve the apps and understand how the Platform is used (using combined, non-identifying data where possible).
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

6. Who we share data with

  • Between passenger and partner, for your ride: the partner sees the passenger's name, pickup and drop; the passenger sees the partner's name, vehicle type and number, phone number for calls, and live location until the trip ends.
  • Trip-sharing links: if you share your live trip, anyone with the link can see the vehicle's location and the ride status. The link does not show names, phone numbers or the ride PIN.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract: cloud hosting and databases, maps, places and route services, SMS / WhatsApp verification, push-notification services and payment gateways. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities: when required by law, court order or a valid request from police or other government bodies, or to protect the safety of a person.
  • Business changes: if rydap's business is transferred or restructured, your data may move with it under this policy.

7. How long we keep data

  • We keep your account data while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, it is closed at once: you are signed out and your profile is removed from the live service. A copy of your account details (for driver partners, also identity and vehicle details) is kept in a restricted archive, used only for safety, fraud prevention, disputes and legal requirements, for as long as those purposes or Indian law require.
  • Some records — such as ride, payment, tax and accounting records — are kept for as long as Indian law requires, and records needed for safety, fraud prevention or disputes are kept until they are resolved.
  • Crash reports and technical logs are kept only as long as needed to fix problems.

8. How we protect data

We use reasonable security practices to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), signed sign-in tokens, one phone per account, access limited to people who need it, and secured hosting. No system is completely secure, so please keep your phone and OTPs private and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you can:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how we use it.
  • Correct or update inaccurate data — many details can be changed in the app.
  • Delete your account and data — in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or see Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent — for example by turning off location or notifications, or deleting your account. Some features will then stop working.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity.

To use these rights, write to support@rydap.in from your registered details. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Children

The Platform is meant for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. Children may travel as passengers only together with an adult who books and takes responsibility for the ride. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Our website

The website rydap.in does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you use the contact form, it opens your own email app with your message; we receive what you send by email.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Platform changes or the law requires. When we make important changes we will tell you in the app or on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.

13. Contact and grievances

For any privacy question, request or complaint, write to support@rydap.in. We aim to acknowledge within 48 hours and resolve within 15 days.
