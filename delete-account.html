Delete your rydap account

You can delete your account at any time — from the app, or by writing to us.

Delete from the app (fastest)

rydap (passengers)rydap Drive (driver partners)
  1. Open the rydap app.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  1. Open the rydap Drive app.
  2. Open your Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is closed right away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email support@rydap.in from any email address with the subject “Delete my account”, your name and your registered mobile number, and whether you use rydap or rydap Drive. We may confirm the request with your registered number. Email requests are handled within 7 business days.

What happens to your data

  • Your profile is removed from the live service and you can no longer sign in to that account.
  • Some records are kept in a restricted archive where Indian law or safety requires it — for example ride, payment, tax and accounting records, and details needed for fraud prevention or open disputes.
  • Driver partners: any amount payable to rydap (wallet dues) remains payable after the account is closed.

Read more in our Privacy Policy.
